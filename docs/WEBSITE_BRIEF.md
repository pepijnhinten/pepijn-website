# Integral Care — Website Brief

## Purpose of the website

The Integral Care website is primarily a professional one-page website aimed at:

1. healthcare and social-care organisations;
2. staffing, intermediary and mediation agencies;
3. recruiters and workforce planners.

During the initial phase, Integral Care will mainly work for healthcare organisations through intermediary agencies.

Direct assignments from healthcare and social-care organisations are also possible.

Wmo-PGB support is a potential future additional service. It may be mentioned carefully, but the website must not suggest that Integral Care is already approved or contracted as a PGB provider by every municipality.

Integral Care does not currently offer Wlz-PGB services.

The public-facing website itself should remain written in Dutch unless explicitly changed later.

## Services for organisations

The website must clearly communicate that Integral Care is available for:

### Direct assignments

Healthcare and social-care organisations may engage Integral Care directly.

### Assignments through intermediaries

Integral Care is also available through staffing, intermediary and mediation agencies.

Potential assignments include:

- temporary staffing/support;
- general support work;
- personal support work;
- coordinating support responsibilities;
- complex support situations;
- temporary and flexible assignments.

Be careful with the term "team leader".

Prefer descriptions involving coordination or supporting a team unless formal managerial responsibility can be explicitly substantiated.

## Wmo-PGB

Wmo = Wet maatschappelijke ondersteuning.

Integral Care may in the future provide direct support to adults using a personal budget under the Wmo.

The website may describe this as:

"Begeleiding vanuit een Wmo-PGB"

Make clear that availability depends on:

- the client's formal decision/indication;
- the support granted under that decision;
- requirements imposed by the relevant municipality.

Do not state or imply:

- "erkende Wmo-aanbieder";
- "approved in all municipalities";
- "all PGB arrangements accepted".

Do not offer or imply Wlz-PGB services.

## Pricing

Do not publish fixed hourly rates on the website.

If pricing needs to be mentioned, use wording such as:

"Tarief en inzet in overleg, afhankelijk van aard, duur en omvang van de opdracht."

Internal minimum rates, target rates and pricing strategy are confidential business information and must never be published.

## Privacy

The website must remain privacy-conscious.

Forms must not request:

- medical information;
- diagnoses;
- BSN numbers;
- client records;
- health information.

If a contact form is used, limit it to information such as:

- name;
- organisation;
- email address;
- telephone number;
- general message.

Clearly state that medical information or other sensitive client information must not be submitted through the website.

A privacy statement should ultimately be available.

Only implement a cookie-consent banner if the actual cookies or tracking technologies used require consent.

Do not add unnecessary analytics, tracking or third-party scripts.

## PGB and healthcare claims

Do not make regulatory, legal or healthcare claims that have not been verified.

The website should describe services rather than imply registrations, approvals or statutory authorisations that Integral Care does not yet have.

Wlz-PGB is currently outside the service offering.

---

## Tone and style

The website should feel:

- professional;
- calm;
- human;
- clear;
- practical;
- trustworthy.

Avoid:

- exaggerated marketing language;
- unnecessary healthcare jargon;
- unsupported claims;
- repetitive copy;
- very long text blocks;
- vague claims such as "unique approach" without evidence.

Integral Care should feel experienced and approachable rather than clinical or aggressively commercial.

---

## Preferred one-page structure

Preferred order:

1. Hero / introduction
2. About
3. Support / services
4. Experience & expertise
5. For organisations
6. Wmo-PGB
7. Contact
8. Footer / legal business information

Navigation may use anchors to these sections.

The current design and structure should be preserved where it already works well. Do not redesign solely to match this exact ordering if the existing implementation achieves the same goals more effectively.

---

## Do not publish

The public website and public repository must not contain confidential business documentation such as:

- VOG documents;
- diploma PDFs;
- insurance policies;
- tax documents;
- contracts;
- banking documentation;
- internal contract reviews;
- internal pricing thresholds;
- identity documents;
- client information.

Sensitive operational information belongs in appropriate secure systems, not in the public website or public GitHub repository.
